Scholarships

At UWA India, we are committed to making a world-class education more accessible. We offer a range of scholarships and bursaries designed to recognise academic achievement, support talented students, and provide financial assistance across our programs. Whether you are a high-performing student, applying early, or seeking support to begin your global education journey, our scholarships are designed to help you succeed.

On this page

No page sections found for navigation.

Why apply for a scholarship?

  • Reduce the cost of your tuition fees
  • Be recognised for your academic achievements
  • Access a globally recognised UWA qualification
  • Start your studies with added financial confidence

How to apply

Most scholarships are assessed automatically as part of your application to study at UWA India and do not require a separate application. For select scholarships, additional documentation or criteria may apply. Full details are available on individual scholarship pages.
