Your online learning platform

The Blackboard Ultra Learning Management System (LMS) is the website where you’ll find all of the content for your units. It’s the digital platform where you:

  • Receive unit information and announcements from your Unit Coordinator
  • Access the Unit Outline and weekly schedule for your unit
  • Access lecture slides and notes
  • Watch recorded lectures
  • Access your reading list
  • Submit assignments and get feedback
  • Join discussions with other students

Access the LMS

To access the LMS, we recommend using web browsers Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox. You can also download and use the Blackboard App where you’ll find some useful guides.

Access the LMS

Navigating Units

After accessing the LMS dashboard, locate and select your course from the Units tab. Note that the Content Page in each unit site includes a navigation menu horizontally across the top of the page and Unit Content in the centre.

Blackboard Ultra Student Orientation

Watch the introductory video and self-enrol in our Blackboard Ultra Student Orientation site to learn more.

  1. Log in to the Blackboard LMS.
  2. Access the Student Orientation site.
  3. Select the “submit” button to enrol.

Learning Materials and Recordings

At UWA, content in the LMS is organised into modules according to week or topic. Click on the title of a Module to see the content for that week or topic, such as your Unit Coordinator’s instructions for the weekly tutorial, lecture notes, or practice questions.

There is a dedicated section in your unit site called Unit Resources with links out to the full collection of lecture recordings for your unit and the full list of Unit Readings.

Viewing Grades and Feedback

When your Unit Coordinator releases grades for an assessment, grades will be visible in the LMS Grade Centre tab.

Some assessment types might display your grade to you immediately, such as multiple-choice quizzes.

For longer written assessment types, your Unit Coordinator will also provide feedback which you can view by clicking on the assessment title in Grade Centre.
