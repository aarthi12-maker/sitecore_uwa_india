Your Library team

The University Library supports learning, teaching and research at UWA India. Library & Learning Resource Advisors at Mumbai and Chennai campuses are available to help all UWA India staff and students, with online support from Librarians at the University Library in Perth.

Online Collections

The Library manages Unit Readings. Students can access a list of their unit's readings, such as the unit’s essential textbook, from the Unit Readings link in their unit's Learning Management System (LMS). Essential textbooks for units can also be found on the Textbooks website in advance of LMS units opening.

Through OneSearch, the Library provides all UWA staff and students with access to essential, relevant online academic resources, including full text articles, eBooks, and databases. UWA India staff and students have access to a selection of UWA Library's online resources that are relevant to the disciplines taught at UWA India.

Sign in to OneSearch with your UWA UniID to ensure access to the full text of articles and ebooks, and to save items and searches to My Favourites.

Improve the relevance of your search results in OneSearch by using the All Filters menu at the top of your search results. Use the 'Advanced Search' option to combine words and phrases in order to expand or narrow down your search.

UWA India staff and postgraduate students can sign in to OneSearch to request digital scans of items from UWA Library’s physical collections, within the page limits permitted by Australian copyright law.

ACE and CARS

ACE (Academic Conduct Essentials) and CARS (Communication and Research Skills) are two distinct compulsory units, that you are expected to complete in your first Semester or Trimester of study at UWA. They are designed to help you learn about academic integrity principles and to gain a strong grounding in basic study skills.

Complete them both early in your studies, for a great head start to your studies. Look for them in your LMS.

LMS access and guidance

Referencing

Referencing shows where you have used other people's ideas in your work and is an essential academic skill.

Check out the Library guide “Referencing at UWA: A beginner’s guide” to find out about why you need to reference, how to manage your references, choosing the right style and getting help. Always use the referencing style recommended in your unit outline.

Individual study support

Library & Learning Resource Advisors can help you find information you need for your studies and answer any referencing questions. We can guide you in effectively using library resources and to develop your search techniques.

Library & Learning Resource Advisors are available for individual appointments with students to support you.

Ask for Library support

Teaching Support

Unit Coordinators can create and manage readings lists within the Unit Readings platform. More information for staff about Unit Readings is available on the Unit Readings Support Guide.

Embed Library skills resources for your students in your LMS, from the collection of learning and teaching resources on the Library Information Literacy Skills Toolkit. Unit Coordinators are also very welcome to contact the Library to request support for students in their unit.

See the UWA India staff Intranet for more information about Library services for staff.

Library Study Spaces

UWA India’s Mumbai and Chennai campus libraries are quiet spaces for individual study. You’ll find a quiet, comfortable environment with a variety of seating available.

Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) stations are available in the UWA India libraries. These stations consist of a monitor that you can plug your device into, to use as a second monitor via a USB-C connection which also charges your device.
