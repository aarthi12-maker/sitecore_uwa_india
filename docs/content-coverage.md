## Official source register

- Catalogue and all course facts: https://india.uwa.edu.au/courses and the individual official course links shown on each course detail page.
- Fees: the individual India course pages. Figures are 2026 first-full-year amounts only.
- Scholarships: https://india.uwa.edu.au/scholarships and its linked award pages. Conflicting Early Bird dates are not reproduced; separate-application award pages state 2026/27 applications closed.
- English: https://www.uwa.edu.au/study/how-to-apply/pathways-and-eligibility/english-language-requirements . Course-specific requirements take precedence.
- Employment: https://india.uwa.edu.au/work-with-uwa . Current vacancies remain on the official site.
- Student careers: https://india.uwa.edu.au/current-students/careers-support .
- Contacts and addresses: https://india.uwa.edu.au/contact-us .
- Wellbeing email: https://india.uwa.edu.au/current-students/wellbeing-support/wellbeing-services . Operating-hours email is not an emergency service.
- India emergency 112: https://www.mha.gov.in/emergency-response-support-system-erss .
- Events: https://india.uwa.edu.au/upcoming-events and https://india.uwa.edu.au/whats-new . Conflicting date ranges mean no upcoming dates are inferred.
- India Library guide and OneSearch: https://guides.library.uwa.edu.au/UWAIndia .

## Remaining confirmations

- Campus-specific security numbers, assembly points and after-hours support require confirmation.
- Official event listing dates conflict, so the local calendar has no asserted dates.
- Enquiries prepare email drafts; no CRM or server submission is configured.
- Existing scholarship and postgraduate photos may differ in crop from supplied screenshots.

These are content/integration limitations, not missing screen files. No enquiries are represented as submitted, no future intake dates are invented, and no campus security or after-hours telephone is guessed.
