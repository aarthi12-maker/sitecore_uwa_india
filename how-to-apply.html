Your Journey with UWA India

Applying to UWA India is straightforward. Follow the steps below to get started:

  1. Check your eligibility

    Before you apply, ensure you meet the requirements for your chosen course:

    • Academic entry requirements for your undergraduate or postgraduate degree.
    • English language requirements.

    Please note: entry requirements may vary by course and level of study

    English language requirements

  2. Choose your course

    • Explore UWA India’s range of internationally recognised degrees and choose a course aligned with your interests and career goals.
    • Make a note of your preferred course and intake.

  3. Prepare your documents

    You will typically need:

    • Academic transcripts and certificates
    • English Language test results (if required)
    • A copy of your passport
    • Any additional documents specified for your course (such as a CV or portfolio)

  4. Submit your application

    You can apply in the way that suits you:

    • Apply directly online to UWA India
    • Apply through an authorised UWA India education agent, who can guide you through the process.
    Apply now

  5. Receive your outcome

    Once submitted, your application will be assessed against the entry requirements for your chosen course. If successful, you will receive:

    • A conditional or an unconditional offer
    • An offer letter outlining any requirements and your next steps.

Application FAQs

What documents will I need?

Prepare your academic transcripts and certificates, English language test results (if required), a copy of your passport, and any additional documents specified for your course.

How can I apply?

Apply directly online to UWA India or through an authorised UWA India education agent.

What happens after I apply?

Your application will be assessed against your course’s entry requirements. If successful, you will receive a conditional or unconditional offer and a letter outlining your next steps.
